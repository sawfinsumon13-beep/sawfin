Case Studies

Evidence-Based Case Scenarios

These scenarios are anonymized and educational. They demonstrate process quality, not guaranteed financial outcomes.

Wrong Address ETH Transfer

Challenge: A high-value ETH transfer was sent to a mistyped address after clipboard malware interference.

Approach: Transaction path analysis, address clustering, and exchange touchpoint identification.

Guidance: Structured reporting package prepared for exchange and legal escalation teams.

Compromised Browser Wallet Session

Challenge: User approved malicious smart contract permissions through a spoofed DApp.

Approach: Contract interaction review, allowance checks, and immediate containment checklist.

Guidance: Wallet migration strategy and post-incident operational security controls delivered.

Legacy Keystore Recovery Attempt

Challenge: Client had incomplete backup fragments from an older desktop wallet.

Approach: File structure validation and recovery tool compatibility matrix preparation.

Guidance: Safe test plan for controlled recovery attempts without data loss risk amplification.

Social Engineering Fraud Incident

Challenge: Client shared seed phrase under fake support pressure.

Approach: Immediate incident timeline reconstruction and fund movement trace.

Guidance: Evidence package for reporting plus long-term wallet compartmentalization plan.

Bridge Transfer Confusion

Challenge: User believed assets were lost after a cross-chain bridge transaction delay.

Approach: Multi-network transaction verification and bridge status interpretation.

Guidance: Resolution path clarified, including safe waiting periods and support escalation checkpoints.

Institutional Wallet Process Review

Challenge: Team needed to reduce single-point key exposure after near-miss phishing event.

Approach: Governance flow analysis and wallet policy hardening recommendations.

Guidance: Multi-layer safeguards implemented with role-based approval processes.

Transparency Commitment

Outcomes in blockchain-related cases are constrained by immutable transaction logic, counterparty cooperation, and case-specific facts. We provide technical clarity and strategic guidance, not guaranteed recovery.
