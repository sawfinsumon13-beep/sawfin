Our Services

Full-service engine support — sourcing, bench testing, EU logistics, swap kits, and after-sales care.

Engine Sourcing

Access to 3,100+ tested BMW engines across all generations. Our procurement network spans salvage yards, fleet decommissioning, and private collections across Germany and Austria.

Dyno Testing

Every engine is run on our SuperFlow dynamometer. We record compression, leak-down, oil pressure, and running parameters. Full test reports included with every purchase.

EU-Wide Shipping

Palletised engine transport to all 28 EU member states. Includes steel frame mounting, protective wrapping, transit insurance, and full customs documentation for non-EU destinations.

Engine Rebuilds

Full and partial rebuilds for S54, S55, N54, N55, M54, and M57 engines. New bearings, seals, timing components, and gaskets. Machine work performed in-house.

6-Month Warranty

Comprehensive mechanical warranty on all tested engines. Covers internal components against pre-existing defects. Extended warranty available for commercial and fleet customers.

Technical Consultation

Free 15-minute consultation for fitment, compatibility, and project planning. Our master technicians advise on engine selection, swap feasibility, and emissions compliance.

ECU Programming

DME coding and immobiliser adaptation for engine swaps. CAS alignment, instrument cluster coding, and EWS deletion for track and competition applications.

Core Exchange

Return your old engine for a core credit. We accept BMW engine cores in any condition. Credit applied toward future purchases or refunded after inspection.

Workshop Training

On-site and remote training for independent workshops. BMW engine identification, diagnostic procedures, and swap installation best practices.
