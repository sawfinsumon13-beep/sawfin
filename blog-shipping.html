Shipping BMW Engines Across Europe

Crating, timelines, and customs guidance for EU and UK buyers.

All engines leave Bavarian Engines on secure pallets or in wooden crates with commercial invoices and tracking. Typical EU road delivery is 3–5 business days after dispatch.

Delivery by region

  • Germany & neighbouring EU: 2–5 business days
  • UK & Ireland: 4–8 business days (customs dependent)
  • Southern / Eastern EU: 5–10 business days
  • Worldwide: quoted case-by-case

On arrival

Photograph the crate before opening, verify the engine stamp matches your invoice, and check for transport damage. Reserve workshop lift time and plan oil priming before first start.
