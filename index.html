Our Story

Founded in 2010, Purebred Kitties was born from a simple belief: every family deserves a healthy, ethically bred kitten — and every breeder deserves a platform built on trust.

We've built a network of 200+ vetted breeders across North America, connecting loving families with their perfect feline companions. We never sell to pet stores. Every kitten is raised in a home, not a cage.
