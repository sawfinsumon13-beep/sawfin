Bred with love,
placed with care

Since 2010, Purrfect Pals has matched over 2,400 families with their ideal feline companions. We are a small, family-run cattery dedicated to ethical breeding practices and the wellbeing of every cat in our care.

Each kitten spends their first 12 weeks in our home, learning to trust humans, play gently, and use a litter box. We never sell to pet stores — only directly to loving families.

  • TICA & CFA registered breeders
  • Lifetime breeder support hotline
  • Starter kit included with every adoption
