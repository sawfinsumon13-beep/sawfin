The BMW M57 inline-six diesel has become the default torque upgrade for Land Rover Defender builds, Mitsubishi Pajero expedition vehicles, Nissan Patrol Y61 platforms, and selected commercial van projects across Europe. Unlike anonymous scrap-yard pulls, Bavarian Engines supplies M57 swap kits with documented donor history, tested engines, vehicle-specific mounts, gearbox adapters, ECU and wiring looms, and workshop documentation — backed by WhatsApp support from technicians who handle these conversions weekly.
