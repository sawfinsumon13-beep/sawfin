Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to common Ethereum recovery and investigation questions.

Can Ethereum transactions be reversed?

No. Ethereum transactions are generally irreversible once confirmed, which is why rapid investigation and reporting are critical.

Do you guarantee successful fund recovery?

No. We provide consultation, investigation, and evidence-backed guidance only. No legitimate provider can guarantee recovery.

What information should I include in my case submission?

Include wallet type, transaction hash, timeline, device context, screenshots, and any prior recovery attempts.

Is my personal information kept confidential?

Yes. We operate using privacy-first, minimum-necessary data practices and secure communication procedures.

Can you help if I sent ETH to the wrong address?

Yes, we can investigate transaction routing, identify interaction points, and provide reporting guidance.

Do you request seed phrases or private keys?

No. You should never share seed phrases or private keys with any service provider.

Can you support legal and compliance reporting?

We provide technical documentation guidance that can be used by legal counsel or compliance teams.
