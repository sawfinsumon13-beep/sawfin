N47 vs N57 — Which BMW Diesel Fits Your Car?

Choosing between N47 and N57 platforms for your replacement engine.

The N47 is a 2.0-litre four-cylinder diesel commonly used in 118d, 120d, 320d, 520d and many X1/X3 variants. The N57 is a 3.0-litre six-cylinder found in 530d, 535d, X5 and X6 — delivering noticeably more torque for heavier vehicles and towing.

Key differences

  • N47 — economical daily-driver rebuilds; suffix letters (N47D20A vs N47D20C) are critical
  • N57 — six-cylinder torque; often sold with turbo, injectors and DME as a package
  • Neither is interchangeable by badge alone — always match VIN and engine stamp

When to choose N47

F30/F20 320d failures, E90 320d timing-chain-era replacements, and budget-conscious daily driver rebuilds where fuel economy matters.

When to choose N57

F10 530d, E90 330d, X5 towing applications, and any chassis that originally shipped with the six-cylinder diesel platform.
