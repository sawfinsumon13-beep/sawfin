Policies

Warranty terms, shipping policy, returns, and privacy information.

Warranty Policy

All tested engines sold by Premium BMW Engines include a 6-month mechanical warranty covering internal engine components against pre-existing manufacturing defects and mechanical faults documented at the time of sale.

The warranty covers: crankshaft, connecting rods, pistons, piston rings, cylinder bores, camshaft(s), valves, valve springs, timing components, oil pump, and water pump (engine-mounted). External components including turbochargers, injectors, sensors, wiring looms, and ancillaries are covered for 30 days from delivery.

Warranty claims must be submitted in writing to flashkingpro202@gmail.com with photographic evidence, diagnostic fault codes, and compression test results. Our technical team will assess the claim within 5 business days. Approved claims are resolved by repair, replacement engine, or pro-rata refund at our discretion.

The warranty is void if: the engine has been modified, overheated due to incorrect installation, run without proper lubrication, or used in competitive motorsport without prior written agreement. Installation must be performed by a qualified technician following BMW repair procedures.

Shipping Policy

Engines are shipped on custom-built steel transport frames, wrapped in protective sheeting, and secured to standard EUR pallets. All shipments within the European Union include transit insurance for the full purchase value.

Standard delivery times: Germany 1–2 business days, EU mainland 3–5 business days, UK/Switzerland/Norway 5–7 business days. Express shipping is available on request. Delivery is to kerbside; forklift or engine hoist access at the delivery address is the customer's responsibility.

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination zone and engine weight. Free shipping is available on orders exceeding €5,000 within Germany. Tracking information is provided via email upon dispatch.

Returns & Refunds

Due to the nature of engine products, returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery only if the engine has not been installed, modified, or run. The engine must be returned in the same condition and packaging as received. Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the buyer.

A 15% restocking fee applies to all returns unless the engine was misrepresented in our listing. Refunds are processed within 10 business days of receiving and inspecting the returned engine.

Core exchange credits are issued after inspection of the returned core engine, typically within 7 business days. Cores must be complete (block, head, sump) and free of catastrophic damage.

Payment Terms

We accept bank transfer (SEPA), PayPal (for orders under €3,000), and major credit cards. A 30% deposit is required to reserve an engine; the balance is due before dispatch. Fleet and commercial customers may apply for 30-day payment terms after credit approval.
