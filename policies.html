Policies

Order, Shipping, and Warranty Policies

Clear policies protect both private buyers and workshops. Every order is processed with documented compatibility, packing, and support conditions.

Compatibility Policy

VIN and engine code confirmation is strongly recommended before invoice and installation.

  • - Suffix-level matching guidance before payment
  • - Buyer confirmation of chassis and intended use

Shipping Policy

All engines are prepared with industrial packaging and dispatched with tracking details.

  • - Crated or palletized dispatch as required
  • - Export-document support for international orders

Warranty Policy

Written warranty guidance is provided in order documents before transaction completion.

  • - Terms shared in writing before final confirmation
  • - Structured support path for valid claims

Returns Policy

Uninstalled units are reviewed under agreed policy terms and documented case handling.

  • - Return reviews based on agreed order conditions
  • - Documentation-first process for faster resolution
