Warranty Policy

All engines sold by Bavarian Engines include a 6-month standard warranty on mechanical integrity for correctly installed units. Extended warranty options are available on request for selected low-mileage units.

What is covered

  • Internal mechanical failure of the engine block, head, and core internals
  • Defects present at time of dispatch but not caused by installation error
  • Units installed according to manufacturer procedures with correct fluids and priming

What is not covered

  • Incorrect suffix code ordered against our advice
  • Damage from improper installation, overheating, or lack of oil
  • External ancillaries (turbo, injectors, sensors) unless stated on the listing
  • Labour costs for removal and refitting

To make a warranty claim, contact us with your order reference, installation date, mileage at fitment, and clear photos or video. Do not strip or modify the engine before we advise.

Return & Refund Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority. Returns are accepted within 30 days of the delivery date, subject to the conditions below.

Eligibility

  • Engine must be unused, uninstalled, and in the same condition as received
  • All returns require prior authorization — contact us before shipping any item back
  • Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the return is due to our error

Non-returnable items

  • Engines that have been disassembled, modified, or installed
  • Items damaged due to improper handling or incorrect installation
  • Special-order or custom-request items, unless defective

Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method. Shipping fees are non-refundable.

Shipping Policy

Shipping is available worldwide from our Hamburg warehouse. Engines are palletised or crated with commercial invoices and tracking.

  • EU delivery: typically 3–5 business days after dispatch
  • UK & Ireland: 4–8 business days (customs dependent)
  • International: 7–14 business days depending on destination and customs

Out-of-stock or special-order items will be confirmed with an approximate ETA via email. Delivery to residential addresses vs workshops with forklift access may affect pricing.
