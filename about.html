Our process

From Donor To Your Workshop

1

Sourcing & identification

Donor vehicles evaluated for mileage and service history. Engine codes stamped, photographed, and matched to the correct BMW platform.

2

Testing & documentation

Mechanical checks and compression tests support honest listings. Workshops receive the data they need for accurate customer quotes.

3

Dispatch & support

Engines palletised or crated for freight with tracking and paperwork. Our team remains available after delivery for warranty questions.
