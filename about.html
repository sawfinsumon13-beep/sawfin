Preserving Original Bavarian Engineering for the Next Generation

Original Bavarian Engine was founded by BMW restoration specialists with one strict principle: supply only authentic original BMW engines and vintage powertrain parts. Every unit is sourced through trusted European networks and verified by technical documentation before listing.

We support private collectors, motorsport historians, and high-end restoration workshops that demand period-correct components with uncompromising authenticity.
