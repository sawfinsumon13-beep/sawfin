Who we are

Apex Bioreagents is a U.S.-based biotechnology supplier focused on recombinant peptides, proteins, antibodies, and preformed fibrils. Our team includes protein chemists, fermentation specialists, and account scientists who understand the constraints of funded research timelines.

Inspired by the product breadth of leading neurodegeneration suppliers, we designed our catalog and services around what laboratories actually run week to week: aggregation assays, seeding experiments, antibody characterization, and structural studies requiring labeled material.
