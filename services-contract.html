Custom Peptides & Proteins

Apex Bioreagents has expertise and production methods that allow us to skillfully produce peptides and proteins that are difficult to express or purify. We can customize products for you whether they be native sequences, mutations, or isotopically labeled.

Services available but not limited to:

  • Molecular biology / gene synthesis support
  • Protein expression (bacterial and related systems)
  • Fermentation scale-up
  • Protein purification
  • Uniform 13C and 15N isotopic labeling of peptides and proteins
  • Mutant and fragment design
  • Analytical QC documentation (COA, identity, purity)

We will take on your contract research projects from start to finish. Contact us at sales@researchpeptidesbio.com with your product needs and we will respond with a custom quote within 24–72 business hours. All projects are carried out in a collaborative manner and complete confidentiality is assured.
