Land Rover Defender M57 Conversion Cost

Budgeting a Defender M57 swap — kits, labour, and long-term reliability.

M57 Defender conversions are among the most popular expedition powertrain upgrades in Europe. Total project cost depends on kit completeness, gearbox choice (manual vs 8-speed auto), and workshop labour.

Typical cost breakdown

  • Complete M57 swap kit: €5,500–€7,200 depending on Defender generation and transmission
  • Professional installation labour: varies by country and workshop
  • Cooling, exhaust, and driveline refresh items during install

Why owners choose M57

Proven torque, excellent parts availability across Europe, and strong support from suppliers like Bavarian Engines Hamburg who stock complete kits with documentation.
