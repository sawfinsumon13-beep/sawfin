About VeloCore

Europe's premier supplier of premium BMW engines

Founded in Amsterdam, VeloCore has spent over 15 years building a reputation as Europe's most trusted source for premium BMW replacement engines. We serve workshops, fleet managers, and individual customers across 48 countries.

Every engine in our inventory undergoes rigorous multi-point inspection and compression testing. We provide full documentation with every sale, including detailed test reports and compatibility verification.

Our Mission

To deliver genuine, inspected BMW engines with transparent pricing, expert support, and industry-leading warranty coverage — making engine replacement simple and trustworthy.
